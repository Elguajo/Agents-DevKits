# Design review rubric

Review the following dimensions in context rather than assigning a synthetic universal score:

1. Hierarchy: important actions and information are discoverable in the right order.
2. Usability: labels, feedback, errors, and recovery support intended tasks.
3. Consistency: tokens, primitives, interaction patterns, and language agree across the scope.
4. Responsiveness: layout and information priority remain usable at supported viewports.
5. Accessibility: the visual treatment does not obscure semantic, keyboard, focus, contrast, motion, or target-size concerns.
6. Performance: visual choices do not imply avoidable layout shift, blocking assets, or costly animation.

Findings should identify the observed problem, affected user, evidence, severity, and a concrete recommended direction.
