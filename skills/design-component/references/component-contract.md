# Component contract checklist

Use this reference when writing a component specification.

Include:

- intent and non-goals;
- anatomy and content slots;
- variants, sizes, and token roles;
- default, hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, error, and selected states when applicable;
- semantic element, accessible name, keyboard model, focus behavior, and live announcements where relevant;
- responsive, overflow, localization, empty-data, and failure behavior;
- the compatibility impact of new props or variants.

The contract describes expected behavior. Rendered checks and expert review remain separate evidence.
